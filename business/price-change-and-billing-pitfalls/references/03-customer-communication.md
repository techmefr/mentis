# price-change-and-billing-pitfalls §3 — The customer notice

A notice fails when the customer has to work out what it means for them, or finds the change on the invoice. The
notice answers the customer's questions, in the order the customer asks them.

## 3.1 What it says
1. **What changes**: the old and the new price, for this customer's plan, in the currency and billing period they
   are billed in, with taxes stated as they appear on their invoice (`skills/vat-handling-in-software-pitfalls`).
2. **When**: the date the new price starts and the date of the first invoice at the new price. Not "soon".
3. **What it means for this customer**: their own next invoice, not a table of all plans. If the notice is
   generic, say so and link to the account page where the figure is.
4. **What they can choose**: stay until a date (§2), change plan, change billing period, cancel, and what each
   costs or refunds. Include every option that exists, not only the one the company prefers.
5. **Why**: a reason the customer can recognise, in terms of value delivered or costs they can see. Do not invent
   a reason, and do not claim a value that no one measured (`business/marketing-claim-substantiation-pitfalls`).
6. **Who to ask**: a named person or a team address that is read.

## 3.2 Timing and repetition
Send it before the change, not with it, and send a reminder before the first invoice. How early is a decision
that depends on the contracts (§2.3) and the billing periods; the notice period required by a given contract or
law is read, not assumed.

## 3.3 Tone and honesty
Write it from a named person. Do not present a forced move as a favour, a gift or "an exciting update". Do not
bury the price in a list of new features: the first paragraph says there is a price change.

## 3.4 Who receives it
Account owners and billing contacts, not every user of the account. Whether a given address may receive a
message of this kind is a data-protection question (`business/data-protection`). The release note about the
features is a different message (`business/release-communication`); link it, do not merge the two.

## 3.5 After sending
Count replies and cancellations by cause and feed them to the read of §1.2. A notice whose bounce rate or
unread rate nobody looked at has not been delivered.
