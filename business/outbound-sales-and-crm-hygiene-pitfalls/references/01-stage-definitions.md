# outbound-sales-and-crm-hygiene-pitfalls §1 — Stage definitions

A pipeline figure is only as good as the sentence behind each stage. When every rep reads a stage differently,
conversion between stages is noise and forecasts are opinions with a number on them.

## 1.1 Entry and exit evidence
1. **Write an entry and an exit for each stage as evidence**, not as a mood: a meeting held, a named
   decision-maker recorded, a written requirement, a quote sent. Symptom: two reps put the same deal in different
   stages. Check: ask a second person to place five real deals using only the table. Fix: rewrite the stages they
   disagreed on.
2. **One owner per stage**, the person who answers for the data. A stage owned by "sales" is owned by nobody.
3. **Required fields belong to a stage**, asked when the evidence exists. A field required too early is filled
   with a placeholder, which is worse than empty.

## 1.2 Moving and stalling
1. **Skipping a stage is a signal, not a shortcut.** Flag a deal that jumps over a stage and ask what evidence
   replaced it. Symptom: stage conversion looks excellent because the hard stage is bypassed.
2. **A pushed close date carries a reason.** A silent push repeated every month is a forecast that never
   closes. Check: count how many times a date moved. Fix: after a stated number of pushes, move the deal back
   or close it; the number is the sales owner's decision, not a figure to copy.
3. **Time in stage is read against the deal's own history**, not against a figure from an article. Say how it
   was computed.

## 1.3 Outbound contacts are not deals
1. **Give contacts their own states** before any deal exists (targeted, in sequence, replied, meeting booked,
   meeting held). Symptom: opportunities created from a click, inflating the pipeline.
2. **Record the source and the touch that produced the reply**, so a meeting can be traced to a message and a
   channel. Without it, attribution is a guess made at report time.

## 1.4 Loss reasons
1. **Closed-lost needs a reason from a short fixed list plus free text**, filled at the moment of loss.
   A list that grows a new value per deal cannot be counted.
2. **Do not average across different causes.** A loss for price, for timing and for a missing feature are three
   problems; report them separately.
