## Removed (not carried, on purpose)
1. **Everything legal**: the FTC substantiation policy, the EU unfair commercial practices directive and
   the 2024 EU consumer-protection directive named in the registry were not opened. The block says claim law varies and was not
   verified.
2. **ContentForge's claims about its own results** (not carried), its confidence percentages and its
   internal gate numbers (80 percent verified and similar) as rules.
3. **Unsourced KPI figures** from the review's source list.
4. **Vendor-coupled lines**: tool calls (web fetch and search instructions), file paths, phase names and
   orchestrator behaviour of the ContentForge pipeline, and the model-recommendation sections of the
   pmalliance skill.
5. **Content-angle and outline-source checks** of the fact-checker: specific to its content pipeline.

## Not verified
1. **Whether the verdict scheme works in practice**: it was never run on a real set of material.
2. **Any claim law**, in any country.
3. **The usage-record and lifetime ideas**: from a design the authors did not build.
4. **Current text of the sources**: they may have changed after the read date; check with
   `skills/source-freshness`.
5. **Statistics and sources in the sources' own worked examples** are marked there as fabricated for
   illustration; none was carried.
