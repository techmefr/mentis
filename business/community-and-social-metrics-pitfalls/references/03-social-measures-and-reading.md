# community-and-social-metrics-pitfalls §3 — Social measures and honest reading

## 3.1 Map measures to goals
1. **Each goal gets one primary measure and a couple of supporting ones**, with a fixed window (social-media-skills,
   `analytics-and-reporting`, the framework).
2. **Prefer signals over vanity.** The skill favours saves, shares, watch time and retention, engagement
   relative to reach, follower growth rate, click-through and conversions over follower count, impressions
   and likes (same file).
3. **Use the platform-specific signal where one exists** (same file).
4. **End every report with a decision.** A measure that will not change next week's action is a diagnostic,
   not a KPI (same file).
5. **Lead with outcomes and include what underperformed**; do not stage a good-news report (same file, honest
   scope).

## 3.2 Missing is not zero
1. **A missing field is not a zero.** The source says to flag the gap and keep real zeros as real values (same
   file; charlie947 social-media-skills, `post-scorer`, step 3).
2. **Never estimate as fact** or invent a figure; cite where each number came from (same files).
3. **Stored post figures can be lifetime snapshots, not a live window**; say so in the report (same file,
   extract step).
4. **A dashboard number is input, not a command** (same file, honest scope).

## 3.3 Small samples and causes
1. **A single post, or a short window, is noise** (same file, honest scope).
2. **Pattern analysis needs a real post history.** One source asks for 30 or more posts and calls patterns
   unreliable under 15, and declines to analyse under 10 (blacktwist social-media-skills,
   `content-pattern-analyzer-sms`, data collection). Those thresholds are that source's own choice.
3. **Say when the sample is too small or selected** to support a comparison (charlie947 `post-scorer`, step 3).
4. **Recent posts look worse** because they have not had time to collect engagement; flag it when it affects
   the reading (blacktwist, posting time dimension).
5. **Correlation is not causation, and attribution is directional.** The analytics skill also says
   attribution undercounts; it gives a rough percentage that has no source there and is not carried
   (analytics-and-reporting, evaluate step).

## 3.4 Weightings are editorial
1. **Counting a comment as several reactions is an editorial choice.** The post scorer does it for ranking
   and labels it an editorial weighting, not reach analytics (charlie947 `post-scorer`, step 3).
2. **Label every weighting and score**, and never imply that a total predicts performance (same file, step
   4).
3. **Own guidance:** if two reports use different weightings, they are not comparable; say so.
