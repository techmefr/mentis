# grant-and-proposal-writing-pitfalls: origin and source stamps

> Provenance of `business/grant-and-proposal-writing-pitfalls`. Read it when a rule has to be traced to its source
> or checked for freshness (`skills/source-freshness`), never to apply a rule.

**Status: 🟡 never dogfooded.** Written from general practice in grant and tender writing, never run on a real call
by us, with no external source. It structures a check; it certifies nothing.

## Verified
The submission, admissibility and page-limit points in the table. They concern two portals; other funders differ.

## Left out on purpose
Any funder's rules, scoring scales, page limits, rates or thresholds. Eligibility, State-aid, intellectual-property
and liability analysis (legal, not covered). Templates of a particular funder. Writing style advice beyond
consistency and checkability. Any source with no open licence: not opened.

## Not verified
1. **Only the two portal pages above were read**; the requirements matrix, go or no-go, budget and outcome sections are one
   author's practice. Searched: EU portal manual, US grants portal help, an NIH page (not reachable); no funder
   guidance on narrative or budget with a verified licence was found.
2. **Deadlines, portals and rules vary** by funder and call and were not checked against any call text.
3. **Never run by us** on a real application.
4. **A block with no outside check**: re-read before relying.

## Own guidance (not sourced)
The requirements matrix (§1.1), go or no-go (§1.2), narrative and budget reconciliation (§2), the
activity-output-outcome split (§2.3), the submission timetable and dry run (§3.1, §3.2.1) and the debrief (§3.3).
