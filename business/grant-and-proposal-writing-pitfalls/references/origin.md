## Left out on purpose
Any funder's rules, scoring scales, page limits, rates or thresholds. Eligibility, State-aid, intellectual-property
and liability analysis (legal, not covered). Templates of a particular funder. Writing style advice beyond
consistency and checkability. Any source with no open licence: not opened.

## Not verified
1. **No source was read**; the block is one author's practice and may miss funder-specific practices.
2. **Deadlines, portals and rules vary** by funder and call and were not checked against any call text.
3. **Never run by us** on a real application.
4. **A block with no outside check**: re-read before relying.
