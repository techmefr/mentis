# grant-and-proposal-writing-pitfalls §3 — Submission and after

## 3.1 A week before
1. **Freeze scope, then only fix compliance and errors.** Symptom: new ideas added the night before and a table no
   longer matching its text.
2. **Fix a timetable backward from the deadline**: draft complete, review, signatures, upload, buffer. Add a
   buffer in days, not hours.
3. **Collect signatures and registrations early**: legal representatives away on holiday and expired registrations
   are common causes of failure.

## 3.2 Upload
1. **Open the portal days before** and run an upload with the real files. Symptom: a format or size limit found at
   the last minute. Check whether the portal accepts partial saves and how the deadline time zone is stated.
2. **Submit with time left**; portals slow down or reject near a deadline. Keep the confirmation. The EU manual
   recommends submitting as early as possible and at least two days before the deadline to avoid technical, local
   configuration and congestion problems.
3. **Open the submitted copy** and check it against the matrix: right files, right versions, nothing missing.
4. **Do not submit twice by accident.** Know whether a later upload replaces or adds, and which one counts. In the EU
   portal each new submission replaces the previous one until the call closes, a submitted proposal is sealed and not
   opened before the deadline (so there is no reason to hold back for fear of disclosure), and after the deadline no
   correction or resubmission is possible.
5. **A submission receipt is not acceptance.** The US portal page read separates a rejection notice (errors to fix),
   a validation receipt (package passed) and an automatic reprocessing that can send them out of order; if the messages
   conflict, ask the helpdesk for a case number and use the portal's tracking view. Keep the first receipt.
6. **Keep proof of a failure.** The EU manual allows a complaint about a portal-side failure within a few calendar days
   after closing, and asks for a time-stamped PDF of the proposal and screenshots of the failure. Save a dated copy of
   the final files before the deadline and screenshot any error.

## 3.3 After the result
1. **Request the reviewer comments** when the funder offers them, and record them against the criteria.
2. **Write a debrief within a week**: what scored, what did not, what to reuse. Store the matrix and the
   attachments in one place for the next call.
3. **If won, reread the awarded terms** before spending: reporting dates, eligible costs, audit and
   record-keeping duties, publicity rules. A budget approved is not a licence to spend outside the rules.
4. **If lost, check the claimed reasons against the call** before reapplying with the same text.
