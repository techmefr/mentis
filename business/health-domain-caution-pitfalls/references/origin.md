## Verified
US-agency facts in the table confirm the existing points on disclaimers, testimonials, study fit, anecdotes and software
regulation. They are US references and do not establish what applies where the product is sold.

## Left out on purpose
Any national or regional law, any regulator's classification of products, any list of permitted claims, any
threshold, any emergency number, any clinical guidance. HIPAA-style or health-data-law analysis (legal, not
covered). Medical content itself. Any source with no open licence: not opened.

## Not verified

   openly licensed health-communication guide was found.
2. **Rules vary by place**; none was checked.

4. **Re-read before relying**; have a qualified reviewer check it before it governs a real health product.
