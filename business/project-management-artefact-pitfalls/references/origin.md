## Verified
1. Critical path, float, baseline and its change control, risk analysis ranges, dangling logic (see the table).
2. Owners of the work as the source of estimates (§2.1.4).

## Left out on purpose
Any named framework, certification vocabulary or mandatory template. Numeric scales, overrun ratios and benchmark
figures. Agile-specific ceremonies (backlog and sprint practice sit in `business/product-ownership`). Tool
recommendations. Earned-value formulas.

## Not verified
1. **Only the schedule points listed under Verified were read**; the RAID log, status, closure and escalation points
   are one author's practice. Searched: GAO schedule guide; no permissive source for risk logs or status reporting found.
2. **Rating scales, thresholds and review rhythms** in the references are suggestions to be defined per project,
   not facts.
3. **Never run by us** on a real project.
4. Re-read before relying; add an open-licence source when one is found.
