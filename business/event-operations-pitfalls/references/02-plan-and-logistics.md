# event-operations-pitfalls §2 — Plan and logistics

## 2.1 Before
1. **Build the target list from attendee and speaker lists and past years**, and book short meetings with a fixed
   time and place, ahead of the day. Symptom: a stand with nothing in the diary. Fix: a booked list with a number
   agreed beforehand.
2. **Announce where your audience already is**, with a reason to find you, not only the stand number.
3. **Brief everyone on the stand**: who the targets are, what to ask, what to record, what not to promise, who
   takes over what. Product claims follow `business/product-marketing` §2.
4. **Prepare the material and the capture method**, including a place to note each conversation. Test the scanner or
   form on real badges before travelling.

## 2.2 Suppliers and venue
1. **Read the contract for cancellation, deposits, minimums, date changes, force majeure and what happens if the
   supplier fails**; the owner who signs decides. Symptom: a deposit lost because a date moved. Check: a table of
   dates, amounts and deadlines.
2. **Ask each supplier what is not included** (setup, power, network, storage, security, cleaning, insurance).
3. **Visit or see the venue where possible**: access, signs, capacity, sound, network, rooms for quiet.
4. **Keep a run sheet** with times, owners, a contact for each supplier and a plan for the failures likely to
   happen (late speaker, no network, no-show of the caterer).
5. **Compare quotes on the same list of items**, not on the total.

## 2.3 Accessibility, consent and data
1. **Plan access**: step-free routes, seating, captions or interpretation where needed, quiet space, dietary and
   access needs asked at registration, accessible slides (`business/accessible-documents-pitfalls`).
2. **Say at registration why the data is collected and how it will be used** (`business/data-protection`). Symptom:
   badge scans added to a mailing list without telling anyone. Fix: say it, and keep to it.
3. **Photography and recording**: tell people before, give a way to opt out, and follow the organiser's rules.
4. **Delete registration data at the end of its stated period.**

## 2.4 Budget
1. **List all costs, not only the fee**: travel, material, staff time, shipping, contingency. Compare with the
   goal in §1.1.
2. **Keep a view of what can be cancelled and when**, and by whom.
3. **Track actual against plan after**, and keep the figures for the next choice.
