## Verified
The owned, rented and borrowed grouping and the readiness gate (simple, lovable, complete, with its two failure modes) match the launch
skill. The staging idea matches its five phases. The launch skill does not cover briefing sales and support, a post-launch
review or a measurement period, so §2.3.1 and §3 stay own guidance.

## Left out on purpose
Every example company and numeric claim in the source. Platform tactics for named networks (volatile). Directory and
launch-site submission advice. Revenue forecasts, sizing and benchmarks. Pricing (own block). Any source with no
open licence, including a product-management skills repository under a non-commercial licence: not opened.

## Not verified
1. **Four skills of one repository were read** (not their reference files); the second repository was not used.
2. **The ICP section rests partly on one customer-research skill**; disqualifiers and the profile excluding companies are
   own practice, and the buying-committee mapping beyond five role names was not found in the source.
3. **Never run by us** on a real launch.
4. **Re-read before relying**: the staging idea and the channel taxonomy, which come from one author's framing.

## Own guidance (not sourced)
Disqualifiers (§1.1), enablement briefing before the public (§2.3.1), the single dated plan (§2.3.4), the goal set before
launch and the review sorted by cause (§3), and the measurement period matched to the sales cycle (§3.1).
