# b2b-go-to-market-pitfalls §3 — After the launch

## 3.1 What to measure
1. **Set the goal and its measure before the launch**: qualified conversations, trials started, activation, deals
   opened. Symptom: success defined afterwards from whatever went up. Fix: write the goal, the data source and the
   review date in the plan.
2. **Look at steps beyond the first visit**: a spike of visits with no activation is a finding about the product
   or the message, not a result.
3. **Measure over a period matched to the sales cycle.** A launch with a long cycle cannot be judged in a week;
   say so, and name the leading signals to watch meanwhile.
4. **Do not rely on attribution alone**: first or last touch hides the other channels. Ask new customers how they
   heard, and record it beside the tool's answer (`business/ad-measurement-pitfalls`).

## 3.2 Review
1. **Hold a review with marketing, sales, support and product**, on a date set in advance. Use the same facts for
   all: what was planned, what happened, what the field heard.
2. **Sort findings into message, audience, channel, product and execution.** Symptom: every miss blamed on
   execution. Fix: one owner and one change per finding.
3. **Record what sales and support heard** from buyers in the buyers' words, and feed it back into the profile
   (§1.2).
4. **Stop what did not work and write down that you stopped it**, so the next launch does not repeat it.

## 3.3 Keep launching
1. **Treat later releases as small launches** with the same gate and handoff, scaled to size.
2. **Update the profile and the claims list** at each review, with the date.
3. **Keep the plan with its review** so a later team can see what was tried.
