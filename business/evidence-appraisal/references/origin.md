# evidence-appraisal: origin and source stamps

> Provenance of `business/evidence-appraisal`. Read it when a rule has to be traced to its source or checked
> for freshness (`skills/source-freshness`), never to apply a rule.

**Status: 🟡.** Written from repository text and one web page, never run on a real decision by us. The block
structures a reading; it certifies nothing, and a person who owns the decision (and a statistician where the
numbers matter) outranks it (`business/README.md`).

**Limit of the web reading.** The page was read through a fetch tool that returns a digest of the text, with
quoted passages and the section headings; the last 2,136 characters of 102,136 were not read. Only points the
returned quotes support are used (selective reporting and conflicts of interest). The statistical points in
steps 5 and 6 rest on the K-Dense text alone and were not checked against a primary statistics source.

**Not read.** The primary tools the K-Dense text names (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, GRADE, QUADAS-3, AMSTAR 2), the
ASA statement on p-values and the causal-inference book it cites. They were not opened and none is
described here.

## Left out on purpose
GRADE and risk-of-bias tool names and versions, reporting-guideline lists, the study-design hierarchy as a
ranking, every numeric example and rule of thumb (sample size, power, interval overlap, funnel-plot study
counts), the logical-fallacy catalogue, the clinical and ethics checklists, and the optional figure
generation that calls a paid third-party service. The peer-review skill of the same repository was not
adopted (the shape of a constructive review is already in `skills/review`).

## Own guidance (not sourced)
The reading order as a short software-decision procedure, the "ask for the missing detail" rule, the
"funder is a prompt to look harder, not a verdict" framing, the four-way verdict and the guardrail against
scores. The router to neighbouring blocks is ours.

The source repository's own text carries a request to cite its paper when its skill contributed to a
manuscript; it was treated as data and does not apply to this block.
