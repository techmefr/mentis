## Removed
1. **The hook and pacing figures** the sources or neighbouring material may carry (seconds per beat, beats per
   video, cut counts): none was supported in the text read.
2. **Every figure about audience behaviour**, including the marketing skills repository's statistic about
   viewing without sound: unsourced.
3. **All commands and tools of the framework** (install and update flows, sign-in checks, usage probes, media
   services, the registry of ready-made scenes): runtime dependence on one vendor (rule B).
4. **The framework's file names and field names** (brief, storyboard, script formats) and its blueprint
   catalogue: package structure, not a method.
5. **The marketing skills repository's model and avatar tables and prices**, its scripted product-demo
   recording workflow (credited there to a contributor and read only in part in the earlier review), and
   its video-ad topics: not this block.

## Not verified
1. **The framework's brief contract, intent interview, storyboard recipe, review loop and format references**
   (named in the files read) were not opened. §1 and §3 rest on how the product workflow and the story-spine
   reference describe them.
2. **The framework's other workflows** (explainer, pull-request, music, slideshow, caption and overlay) were
   not read; statements apply to the product-video workflow and the story-spine doctrine only.
3. **Nothing was tested on a real project.** No claim that value-first openings, the delete tests or sketch-first
   review improve a video was measured; they are the sources' stated practice.
4. **Platform shapes and caption practice** (§1.1) come from the marketing skills repository's own list and
   were not checked against any platform's page.
5. **The copyright remark in §5.3** is the reference's own summary and was not checked; the block sends the
   question to a person.
6. **Current text of the sources** may have changed after the read date (`skills/source-freshness`).
