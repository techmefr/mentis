## Removed
1. **Every deadline and threshold.** The regulation's application date, the incident reporting time limits,
   materiality thresholds and register templates are set elsewhere or were not used as rules; none is stated.
2. **Chapter IV (resilience testing) and Chapter V (oversight of critical providers, information sharing).**
   Articles 24 to 27 and 31 onward were not read, apart from cross-references seen in the articles above.
3. **Articles 7, 15, 16(3) detail and 21, 22.** Seen, not turned into rules.
4. **Technical standards and delegated acts** under Articles 15, 18, 20, 28 and 30. Not read.

## Not verified
1. **Consolidated version, corrigenda and amending acts** were not checked; the block tells the reader to read
   the current text.
2. **Technical standards** (incident classification and thresholds, reporting content, time limits and
   templates, register templates, subcontracting content) and national transposition or supervisory practice.
3. **Whether any given entity or supplier is in scope**, or critical, and which category applies.
4. **Own guidance, flagged in the text:** the two-field incident record, dummy-incident route tests, the
   register row design, the clause checklist with locations, the audit evidence pack, the exit data export test.
5. **Cross-references to other blocks** are by name; section numbers are cited only where they exist.
