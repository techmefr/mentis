# cra-scope-and-role-determination-pitfalls: origin and source stamps

> Provenance of `skills/cra-scope-and-role-determination-pitfalls`. Read it when a rule has to be traced to its
> source or checked for freshness (`skills/source-freshness`), never to apply a rule.

**Status: 🟡 never dogfooded.** Written from a regulation text, never run by us: no register was built, no
counsel review done. A working method, not legal advice, and it does not replace counsel.

## Removed
1. **Any application date or deadline.** Article 71 was not read for this block; staged dates are left to the
   existing clock block and to counsel.
2. **Any conclusion on whether a given organisation or project is in scope.**
3. **Obligation detail of Articles 13 to 20** beyond headings and the paragraphs cited, and the minimum
   support-period and retention lengths, deliberately not restated.

## Not verified
1. **Annexes III and IV** (important and critical categories), Annex I, Annex VII and the product lists.
2. **Harmonised standards, implementing and delegated acts**, SBOM format, support-period guidance.
3. **National authorities, penalties, transitional provisions** (Articles 64, 69) and the consolidated text and
   amendments after 20.11.2024.
4. **Recitals** on open source and non-commercial supply were not read; the open-source part rests on the
   articles only.
5. **Own guidance, flagged in the text:** register fields, per-product role table, re-run triggers, escalation
   package.
