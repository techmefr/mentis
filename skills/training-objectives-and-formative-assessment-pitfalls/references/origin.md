## Rewrite notes
**Sources disagree: novices and sequence.** Carpentries recommends basic verbs for novices and warns against
skipping steps; idstack says learners need not master facts first and flags strict low-to-high sequences,
citing an evidence tier scheme. The block carries only the shared points and states the rest as a judgment
call (§1.3).

**Examples, not rules.** Five and ten to fifteen minutes between checks, the break and survey timings, and
the table statuses are single-source choices.

## Removed
1. **idstack's evidence tiers and citation codes** ([T1] to [T5], [Alignment-n]): not verified, not carried;
   the claim that higher-order retrieval practice helps is the source's and is not restated as fact.
2. **idstack's expertise-reversal rules by learner level** (advanced learners should skip lower levels):
   only the existence of the disagreement is carried.
3. **Carpentries-specific tooling and programme details**: its survey links and dashboards, its Etherpad,
   Google form and sticky-note colours, the instructor-team and lesson-maintainer parts.
4. **The Dunning-Kruger name** and the "average attention span" claim are the source's explanations and are
   given as the source's, not repeated as findings.
