1. **Any national rule.** No member-state law was read; none is stated.

3. **Annexes I and II (sectors and types).** Not read; the sector question is routed to counsel.

5. **Articles 23(5) to (10)** (authority feedback, information sharing between states, public disclosure). Read on the
   page, not used as rules.

## Not verified
1. **Annexes, definitions and the size ceilings** of the enterprise-size recommendation.
2. **Consolidated version, amendments and corrigenda** after 27.12.2022.
3. **National transposition** in every member state, including when awareness is deemed to start, periods, thresholds,
   channels and liability.
4. **Whether any implementing act** specifies significant incidents or technical requirements for a given entity.
5. **Own guidance, flagged in the text:** the measures-as-evidence table, the supplier register, recording awareness
   time as a case field, named declaring and filing roles with deputies, template and channel preparation, the
   escalation list and the dry run.
