## Removed in the verification pass
1. **Every period and interval in square brackets** (retention of assessment records, archive and anonymise
   delays for the log, review intervals for the conformance affirmation): placeholders with no justification.
2. **The named law firm and the named specialist consultancy**: commercial names, not method.
3. **The statements that the policy is compliant with, or conformant to, a named international standard**, and
   the public-declaration clause: the standard was not read, so nothing here claims conformance.
4. **The appendices** (roles, licences, code acceptance, incident handling, training): the source points to
   spreadsheet tabs that were not part of this reading.
5. **The employment-law drafting** of the acknowledgement clause (the policy is not part of the contract and may
   be varied): not carried.
6. **The 100 KB-plus checklists of the sibling OpenChain CRA-Compliance repository**: not read.

## Not verified
1. **Own guidance, flagged in the text:** small companies combining roles, deciding what "distributed" means
   with counsel, dating each row of the known-licence list, generating the component list from the build,
   recording a missing bundle as a gap, leaving retention and intervals to the owner and counsel, a changes list.
2. **Whether the source's structure meets any standard** was not checked and is not claimed.
3. **Applicability to hosted-only products:** the source is written for distributed software with an optional
   extension; what applies to a hosted service is for counsel.
4. **No lawyer has reviewed this block.**
