# journal-entry-review-pitfalls §2 — Review checklist and recurring errors

> Section 2 of `skills/journal-entry-review-pitfalls`. Read it when an entry is reviewed or audited before the
> period is locked. Sourced from the same journal-entry-prep skill (commit `33eeda1`), sections "Review
> Checklist" and "Common Errors to Check For", read 2026-10-16. Sentences marked "own guidance" are ours.

## The reviewer's checklist
1. **Balanced.** Debits equal credits. The source notes a system should prevent an unbalanced entry and says
   to check manual ones anyway.
2. **Open period.** The entry is not posting into a closed period.
3. **Account fit.** The accounts exist and suit the transaction.
4. **Amounts reproducible.** Each amount is mathematically accurate and backed by the cited calculation.
5. **Description sufficient** for audit use, and **supporting documents** complete and referenced.
6. **Coding right.** Department, cost centre or project coding is correct.
7. **Consistent** with prior periods and with the accounting policies in force.
8. **Reversal set as intended** for entries meant to reverse.
9. **Within the preparer's authority.**
10. **Not a duplicate** of an entry already posted.
11. **Unusual or large amounts explained.**

Own guidance: a checked box means the reviewer opened the support, not that the preparer said so.

## Errors that recur
The source lists twelve; eleven are carried as review prompts.

1. **Unbalanced** entries, even though the system should refuse them.
2. **Wrong period**, including an already closed one.
3. **Flipped sign**: a debit entered as a credit or the reverse.
4. **Duplicates**: the same transaction recorded twice.
5. **Wrong account**, especially between accounts with similar codes.
6. **Missing reversal**, which double counts an entry that was meant to unwind.
7. **Stale recurring estimates** not updated when circumstances changed.
8. **Round-number estimates** that may not reflect an actual calculation.
9. **Wrong exchange rate or rate date** on a foreign-currency entry. Own guidance: say in the entry which rate
   source and which date were used.
10. **Intercompany entry without its counterpart or elimination.** Own guidance: ask the counterparty entity to
    confirm its side before the period locks.
11. **Cut-off error**: a transaction booked in the wrong period relative to the delivery or service date.

## How to use the list
Own guidance: run the checklist first, then read the errors as a second pass on the entries that passed it,
because an entry can tick every box and still be a duplicate or a stale recurring one. Record each finding as
one line (entry, line, what is wrong, what support is missing). The source's eleventh error, the choice between
capitalising and expensing, is an accounting judgement and is left out; ask an accountant.
