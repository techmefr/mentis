## Omnibus status found
Adopted as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, signed 8 July 2026, published in the Official Journal on 24.07.2026, entering
into force on the third day after publication. Seen on the Legislative Observatory file and on the act itself, read
2026-10-19. Nothing later than that act was searched for.

## Removed
1. **Articles 7 to 49 beyond those read, and Articles 52, 54 to 112.** Not read as rules; high-risk requirements,
   conformity assessment, provider and deployer obligations of Chapter III, fundamental rights impact assessments,
   penalties and governance are not stated.
2. **Annexes other than the Annex III headings.** Annex I, XI to XIII and the Annex III sub-points past the first five
   areas were not read.
3. **Amending points not read in full:** the act's points on Articles 10, 11, 17, 25 (as amended), 27, 28 to 30, 40, 42,
   43, 56 to 60, 63 to 77, 95 to 99 and the amendments to the machinery and aviation regulations.
4. **Any national rule or authority.** None read.

## Not verified
1. **Consolidated version, corrigenda and any act after 24.07.2026** that changes the dates or articles read.
2. **Whether the proposal's removal of Article 49(2) registration made it into the act**; Article 49 was read in its
   original form only.
3. **Commission guidelines** on Article 6 and Article 50, codes of practice and harmonised standards: none opened.
4. **Article 26 and the deployer obligations of Chapter III**, and the Article 99 penalty rules as amended.
5. **The computation threshold and criteria** for systemic-risk models, and the delegated acts amending them.
6. **Own guidance, flagged in the text:** the triage order, the inventory fields, the change, incident and literacy logs,
   keeping marketing copy as intended-purpose evidence, and re-reading dates in the session.
